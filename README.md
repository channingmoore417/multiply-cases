# multiply-cases

Landing pages for Multiply — law firm marketing.

Both pages are self-contained static HTML: all CSS, JavaScript, and images
(inline base64 JPEGs) are embedded, with Google Fonts loaded from a CDN. They
can be served by any static host or opened directly in a browser.

## Pages

### `index.html` — VSL landing page

- Hero with video player placeholder and booking CTA
- Booking modal (date/time picker, timezone-aware, lead capture form)
- Case study section with before/after screenshots
- Offer breakdown ($497/mo) and footer

### `booking.html` — standalone "Book A Call" page

Same design system, booking flow presented as a full page rather than a modal.
